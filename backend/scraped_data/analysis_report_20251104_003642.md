## Database Design Notes

Based on the requirements:

### Items Table (Shared)
- Loot, Weapons, and Equipment will share one `items` table
- Include a `type` field to denote: 'loot', 'weapon', 'equipment'
- Items can be used for crafting OR salvaged into other items
- Relationships: many-to-many for crafting and salvaging

### Tasks Table (Shared)
- Quests, Expeditions, and Workshops will share one `tasks` table
- Include a `type` field to denote: 'quest', 'expedition', 'workshop'
- Track components needed (for upgrades/completion)
- Checkable/completable items
